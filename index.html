What NDT does

A trench for your next project. Care for what’s already there.

New services often need to pass through places already occupied by pipes, cables, paths and gardens. GreenVac uses pressurised water to loosen the ground and vacuum extraction to remove it, forming a trench while reducing the risk of damage to existing underground services.

From an electrical run down the side of a house to a plumbing trench through an established garden, we help make difficult access manageable.

Complete trenches for new pipes and cables.NDT creates a route for your new service—not just a small hole to expose an existing one. James will talk through the trench route and site conditions with you.

The compact advantage

You don’t always need a big truck.

Hydro excavation has a place on major construction sites—and beside your house. GreenVac brings that approach to smaller projects with a compact trailer setup and hoses that reach the work area.

Up to 35 metres from the trailer

On suitable sites, we can work up to 35 metres from the trailer, reaching around buildings and into spaces where machine access is difficult.

The trailer doesn’t have to enter your backyard: the hose runs from the setup to the dig.

  • Access down narrow side passages and around established landscaping.
  • Careful excavation around existing underground services.
  • Equipment suited to smaller residential and trade projects.
The benefits of hydro excavation

Safer digging.
Less disruption.

Water and vacuum excavation help reduce the risk of damage to underground pipes and cables, with less disturbance to surrounding gardens and paths. Our hoses reach tight spaces where machine access is difficult, so we can dig complete trenches and work around existing structures.
